How much does it cost to hire a lawyer in Pakistan?

Lawyers' fees depend on the type of case, the court, and how many hearings are expected. MeraLawyer.pk gives you the full fee for your case after a free first call, before you commit to anything.

How does MeraLawyer.pk work?

You tell us about your matter by phone, WhatsApp or the form. A case manager understands it and matches you with a vetted advocate in Islamabad or Rawalpindi who handles that type of case. You agree the fee, and the lawyer takes over.

Are your lawyers licensed?

Yes. Every lawyer we work with is an advocate enrolled with the relevant bar council, and we check credentials before they join.

Which courts do your lawyers appear in?

The Islamabad High Court, the Lahore High Court Rawalpindi Bench, the district and sessions courts of Islamabad and Rawalpindi, family courts, banking courts and tribunals.

Can overseas Pakistanis hire a lawyer through you?

Yes. You can brief a lawyer by WhatsApp or video call and authorise them through a power of attorney attested at a Pakistani embassy or consulate.
