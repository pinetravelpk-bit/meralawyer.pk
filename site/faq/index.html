Family lawyer

How much does a family lawyer charge in Islamabad?

Fees depend on the type of case, whether it is contested and how many hearings are expected. A simple uncontested matter costs far less than a contested custody case. MeraLawyer.pk gives you the full fee for your case after a free first call.

How long does a family case take?

Section 12-A of the Family Courts Act 1964 directs family courts to decide cases within six months of the defendant's first appearance. Contested custody and maintenance cases can take longer in practice.

Can I file a family case if I live abroad?

Yes. You can appoint a lawyer through a power of attorney attested at a Pakistani embassy or consulate, and follow the case by WhatsApp and video call.

Which court hears a khula case?

The Family Court where the wife ordinarily resides, for example the family courts in Islamabad or Rawalpindi.

Criminal lawyer

What is the difference between pre-arrest and post-arrest bail?

Pre-arrest bail (section 498 CrPC) protects you from arrest before it happens and is granted only in limited circumstances, such as a malicious case. Post-arrest bail (section 497 CrPC) is sought after you have been arrested.

Can I get bail on the same day?

A bail petition can often be filed the same day, but the court decides when to hear it. In urgent matters an interim pre-arrest bail may be granted at the first hearing.

What should I do if a false FIR is registered against me?

Do not ignore it. Speak to a criminal lawyer immediately about pre-arrest bail and whether the FIR can be challenged through a quashment petition.

Do you handle FIA cyber crime cases?

Yes. Our criminal lawyers handle complaints and defence in FIA cyber crime cases under PECA 2016.

Property lawyer

How do I verify a property before buying in Rawalpindi or Islamabad?

Ask for the fard (record of rights) or society allotment and transfer documents, then have a lawyer verify them with the land record office or housing society, check for pending litigation, and confirm the seller's identity.

What can I do if someone illegally occupies my property?

You can file a complaint under the Illegal Dispossession Act 2005 and, in parallel, a civil suit for possession. Act quickly and keep proof of ownership.

How is inherited property divided?

Shares are fixed by Islamic inheritance law for Muslims. Legal heirs can agree a family settlement, or any heir can file a partition suit in the civil court.

Corporate lawyer

Do I need a lawyer to register a company?

It is not legally required, but a lawyer or corporate consultant ensures your memorandum and articles of association, shareholding and filings are correct, which prevents problems with banks, FBR and investors later.

Is a sole proprietorship or a private limited company better?

A sole proprietorship is cheaper and faster to set up but you are personally liable for its debts. A private limited company separates your personal assets from the business and is preferred by banks, investors and large clients.

When is the income tax return due in Pakistan?

For individuals and AOPs the due date is usually 30 September each year for the previous tax year, unless the FBR announces an extension.

Lawyer for overseas Pakistanis

Can I fight a property case in Pakistan while living abroad?

Yes. You authorise a lawyer or a trusted relative through a power of attorney attested at the Pakistani embassy or consulate, and your lawyer appears in court for you.

How do I attest a power of attorney abroad?

Sign it before the consular officer at the Pakistani embassy or consulate, or through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs' online e-attestation and consular facilities where available. In Pakistan it may then need to be registered with the sub-registrar for property use.

How will I know what is happening in my case?

Your lawyer sends an update after every hearing by WhatsApp, along with copies of orders when they are issued.

Company registration

How long does company registration take in Pakistan?

When documents are complete, SECP often processes online incorporation within a few working days. Name objections or missing documents add time.

What is the minimum number of directors?

A single-member company needs one director and one nominee. A private limited company needs at least two members and two directors.

Income tax return filing

What is the deadline for filing income tax returns?

The due date for individuals is normally 30 September for the tax year ending 30 June. The FBR sometimes extends it, so check the current year's announcement.

What happens if I file late?

You can still file, but late filing can attract penalties and you may need to pay a surcharge to get onto the Active Taxpayers List for that year.

NTN registration

Is my CNIC number my NTN?

For individuals registered on IRIS, yes: the CNIC number serves as the NTN.

How long does NTN registration take?

Individual registration is often completed the same day once verification codes are received. Business and company NTNs can take longer if documents are requested.

Sales tax registration

Do I register with FBR or PRA?

For goods, the FBR. For services, it depends on where the service is provided: Islamabad services fall under ICT (administered by FBR), Punjab services under the PRA.

Do I have to file a return if I had no sales?

Yes. Registered persons generally must file a monthly return even when it is a nil return.

Trademark registration

How long is a trademark valid in Pakistan?

Ten years from the filing date, renewable for further ten-year periods.

Can I use TM before registration?

Yes, TM can be used to claim a mark. The registered symbol should only be used once the mark is registered.

Legal notice

Is a legal notice compulsory before filing a case?

Not always. It is required before suing the government (section 80 CPC) and before a defamation suit, and it is strongly advisable in most money and contract disputes.

What if the other side ignores the notice?

You can proceed with a civil suit, recovery suit or other action. The notice becomes evidence that you tried to settle.

Agreements and contracts

Does a rent agreement need to be registered?

Leases for more than one year must be registered under the Registration Act 1908. Shorter agreements are commonly made on stamp paper, and some areas also require tenant registration with police.

Is an agreement on plain paper valid?

It can be valid as a contract, but insufficient stamp duty can make it inadmissible as evidence until the duty and penalty are paid.

Court marriage

Can court marriage be done on the same day?

The nikah and affidavits can usually be completed the same day. Union Council registration and the NADRA certificate follow afterwards.

Is court marriage legal without parents' consent?

Under Pakistani law an adult Muslim woman can marry with her own free consent. The nikah must still be properly registered.

Khula

How long does khula take in Pakistan?

The court hearing stage can be quick if the husband does not contest. After the decree, the Union Council process takes about 90 days before the divorce becomes effective.

Do I have to return the haq mehr?

The court may order the wife to return the dower she received, or part of it. Deferred dower that was never paid is usually not returned.

Succession certificate

Which is faster, NADRA or court?

The NADRA route is usually faster when all legal heirs agree. If there is any dispute among heirs, the matter goes to the civil court.

Can a woman heir apply?

Yes. Every legal heir, male or female, is entitled to their share and can participate in the application.

Property verification

Why verify a property if the seller has the original documents?

Fraud through forged or duplicate documents, sales by someone without authority, and properties under a stay order are common. Only the official record confirms the true position.

Should I pay an advance before verification?

Avoid paying a large advance until verification is complete. If you must, use a written agreement with clear refund terms.

Property transfer and registry

What is the difference between registry and mutation?

Registry is the registration of the sale deed with the sub-registrar. Mutation (intiqal) updates the land record so the new owner appears in the fard. Both are needed.

Is a gift deed taxed?

Gifts between close family members are treated differently from sales, but stamp duty and registration fees still apply. We calculate the exact cost for your case.

Power of attorney

Can I sell property through a power of attorney?

Yes, if the POA specifically authorises it and is registered. Buyers and registrars usually require a registered special POA for property sales.

How do I cancel a power of attorney?

Execute a revocation deed, register it where the POA was registered, and notify the attorney and relevant offices.

Bail application

Which court do I apply to for bail?

Usually the Sessions Court first. If bail is refused, you can apply to the High Court.

Is bail available in non-bailable offences?

Yes, bail can be granted in non-bailable offences at the court's discretion. It is harder in offences carrying severe punishment.
