Quick answer: ntn registration

An NTN (National Tax Number) is your registration with the Federal Board of Revenue. For individuals, the NTN is the same as the CNIC number once registered on the FBR's IRIS portal. Businesses, AOPs and companies receive their own NTN. Registration needs a CNIC, a mobile number registered in your own name, an email and an address.

Who needs ntn registration?

Anyone starting a business, freelancers receiving foreign payments, landlords, and anyone who needs to file a tax return.

NTN registration: frequently asked questions

Is my CNIC number my NTN?

For individuals registered on IRIS, yes: the CNIC number serves as the NTN.

How long does NTN registration take?

Individual registration is often completed the same day once verification codes are received. Business and company NTNs can take longer if documents are requested.

Do freelancers need an NTN?

Freelancers receiving foreign remittances benefit from registering and filing returns, and may qualify for reduced tax treatment on IT export income.
