Quick answer: trademark registration

A trademark is registered with the Intellectual Property Organization of Pakistan (IPO Pakistan) under the Trade Marks Ordinance 2001. The process is: search, file an application in the right class, respond to examination, publication in the Trade Marks Journal for opposition, then registration. Registration lasts ten years and can be renewed.

Key facts at a glance

  • Trademarks in Pakistan are registered with the Intellectual Property Organization of Pakistan (IPO-Pakistan) under the Trade Marks Ordinance 2001.
  • Goods and services are classified into international classes, and a separate filing is needed for each class.
  • After examination, an accepted mark is advertised in the Trade Marks Journal, and third parties can oppose it within the time allowed by law.
  • A registered trademark is valid for ten years and can be renewed for further ten-year periods.
  • A search before filing reduces the risk of objection or opposition.

Who needs trademark registration?

Businesses, startups, online brands, restaurants, manufacturers and anyone with a name or logo worth protecting.

Why should you register your trademark?

Your brand name and logo are business assets. Registration gives you the exclusive right to use the mark for the registered goods or services in Pakistan and a stronger legal basis to stop others from copying it. Without registration you may still have rights through use, but proving and enforcing them is harder, slower and more expensive. Registration also helps with online marketplaces, franchising, investment and selling the business later.

What can be registered as a trademark?

A trademark is any sign that distinguishes your goods or services from those of others. It must be distinctive and must not be identical or confusingly similar to an existing mark in the same class.

  • Brand names and business names used as brands
  • Logos and stylised words
  • Slogans and taglines, if distinctive
  • Combinations of words, colours and devices
  • Service marks for service businesses

What is the trademark registration process in Pakistan?

The process at IPO-Pakistan has several stages, and it is normal for it to take many months from filing to registration.

  • Search: check existing marks in the relevant class
  • Filing: submit the application with the mark, class and specification of goods or services
  • Examination: the registry examines distinctiveness and conflicts and may raise objections
  • Reply to objections, if any, in writing or at a hearing
  • Acceptance and advertisement in the Trade Marks Journal
  • Opposition period for third parties
  • Registration certificate issued if there is no successful opposition

How do you choose the right class?

Pakistan follows the international classification of goods and services. A clothing brand files in the class for clothing; a restaurant files in the class for food and drink services; a software company may need classes for software and for IT services. Filing in the wrong class gives no protection where you need it, while filing in too many classes adds cost. Your lawyer will match your business to the right classes.

What documents are needed?

Trademark filing needs only a few documents.

  • Applicant's name and address (individual, firm or company)
  • CNIC of the individual or authorised signatory
  • Company incorporation certificate or partnership deed, if applicable
  • The mark: word, or a clear image of the logo
  • Description of goods or services and date of first use, if already in use
  • Authorisation in favour of the lawyer or agent

What happens if someone copies your trademark?

If your mark is registered, you can send a legal notice, oppose a conflicting application, and file a suit for infringement seeking an injunction and damages. If it is not yet registered, you may still have remedies for passing off based on prior use. Act quickly when you notice copying, and keep evidence such as invoices, advertisements and online listings that show your earlier use.

Trademark registration: frequently asked questions

How long is a trademark valid in Pakistan?

Ten years from the filing date, renewable for further ten-year periods.

Can I use TM before registration?

Yes, TM can be used to claim a mark. The registered symbol should only be used once the mark is registered.

How long does trademark registration take?

It usually takes many months because of examination and the opposition period. You get a filing number immediately, which establishes your priority date.
