Quick answer: agreements and contracts

A well-drafted agreement prevents most disputes. In Pakistan, agreements such as property sale and rent agreements are executed on stamp paper of the correct value, signed before witnesses, and in some cases registered with the sub-registrar. Our lawyers draft and review agreements so your money and rights are protected.

Key facts at a glance

  • Contracts in Pakistan are governed mainly by the Contract Act 1872.
  • Most agreements must be written on stamp paper of the correct value under the Stamp Act 1899; e-stamp paper is used in Islamabad and Punjab.
  • Rent agreements in Islamabad fall under the Islamabad Rent Restriction Ordinance 2001; in Rawalpindi, under the Punjab Rented Premises Act 2009.
  • Some documents, such as certain leases and sale deeds, must be registered with the Sub-Registrar under the Registration Act 1908.
  • A clear dispute-resolution clause can save months of litigation if something goes wrong.

Why should a lawyer draft your agreement instead of a template?

Templates copied from the internet are written for other countries or other deals. They often miss Pakistani requirements such as the correct stamp duty, registration, the right court or arbitration forum, and the specific obligations of your arrangement. When a dispute arises, the court reads what is written, not what the parties intended. A lawyer-drafted agreement records your actual deal clearly enough that a judge, or the other party, cannot misread it.

A good agreement also prevents disputes in the first place. When payment dates, deliverables, penalties and termination rights are spelled out, both sides know what to expect.

What should every agreement include?

Whatever the type of agreement, certain clauses do most of the work when a problem arises. Your lawyer will tailor these to your deal.

  • Full names, CNIC numbers and addresses of the parties
  • A precise description of the property, goods or services
  • Price, payment schedule and method of payment
  • Duration, renewal and termination rights
  • What happens on breach, including penalties or forfeiture of advance
  • Dispute resolution: courts, mediation or arbitration, and the city
  • Signatures of parties and two witnesses, with their CNICs

What are the special rules for rent agreements in the twin cities?

Tenancy law differs between the two cities. In Islamabad, rent matters are governed by the Islamabad Rent Restriction Ordinance 2001 and disputes go to the Rent Controller. In Rawalpindi, the Punjab Rented Premises Act 2009 applies; it requires the tenancy agreement to be in writing and contemplates registration with the Rent Registrar, and disputes go to the Rent Tribunal.

In both cities, landlords are also expected to give tenant details to the local police under the applicable tenant-registration rules. Missing this step can cause problems for the landlord, so your lawyer will remind you to complete it.

Does an agreement need to be on stamp paper or registered?

Most agreements need stamp duty paid under the Stamp Act 1899, usually through e-stamp paper. The amount depends on the type and value of the document. An unstamped or under-stamped document can still be admitted in evidence in some cases, but only after paying the duty and a penalty, so it is better to stamp it correctly at the start.

Registration is a separate question. Sale deeds, gifts of immovable property and leases above certain terms need registration with the Sub-Registrar. An agreement to sell is usually not registered, but a well-drafted one protects the buyer if the seller backs out.

How long does it take to prepare an agreement?

A standard rent or service agreement can usually be drafted within one to two working days once we have the details. Complex shareholder or supply agreements take longer because they need discussion between the parties. Reviewing an agreement someone else has drafted is usually quicker, and we will mark the risky clauses and suggest changes.

Agreements and contracts: frequently asked questions

Does a rent agreement need to be registered?

Leases for more than one year must be registered under the Registration Act 1908. Shorter agreements are commonly made on stamp paper, and some areas also require tenant registration with police.

Is an agreement on plain paper valid?

It can be valid as a contract, but insufficient stamp duty can make it inadmissible as evidence until the duty and penalty are paid.

Can you review an agreement before I sign?

Yes. We review it and point out risky clauses before you sign.
