Quick answer: court marriage

Court marriage in Pakistan is a nikah performed with the free consent of both adults, usually at a lawyer's office, with a nikah registrar and two witnesses. The nikahnama is registered with the Union Council under the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, after which a NADRA computerised marriage certificate can be issued. Both parties must be adults.

Key facts at a glance

  • In Pakistan, a court marriage is a nikah performed with the consent of both adults and registered under the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961.
  • Both parties must be of legal marriageable age; in Islamabad, the minimum age is 18 for both under the ICT Child Marriage Restraint Act 2025.
  • The nikah is registered by a licensed Nikah Registrar and then with the Union Council, after which NADRA can issue a marriage registration certificate.
  • A free-will statement or affidavit of the bride and groom is commonly prepared to record consent.
  • Two adult Muslim witnesses are needed for the nikah.

Who needs court marriage?

Adult couples who want a simple, documented nikah, and couples marrying without a large ceremony.

What does court marriage mean in Pakistan?

The phrase court marriage is commonly used, but the marriage itself is not performed by a judge. It is a nikah conducted with the free consent of both adults, usually arranged through a lawyer who prepares the documents, arranges a licensed Nikah Registrar and witnesses, and makes sure the marriage is properly registered. Lawyers often prepare affidavits of free will so that consent is clearly documented.

Couples choose this route when they want a simple ceremony, when families are not involved, or when they want to be sure the paperwork is correct from the start.

Who can have a court marriage in Islamabad or Rawalpindi?

Any two Muslim adults who are legally eligible to marry each other can have a nikah. Both must consent freely. Legal age requirements apply: in Islamabad, the minimum age for marriage is 18 for both parties under the ICT law enacted in 2025. In Rawalpindi, Punjab law applies, and your lawyer will confirm the current requirement. Marriages involving a non-Muslim party, or a second marriage by a husband, have additional legal requirements.

What is the court marriage procedure step by step?

The process is straightforward when documents are ready. It can often be completed in a single day, with registration following.

  • Consultation and document check by the lawyer
  • Preparation of affidavits or free-will statements of both parties
  • Nikah performed by a licensed Nikah Registrar with two adult Muslim witnesses
  • Completion and signing of the nikah nama, including the haq mehr and delegated rights
  • Registration of the nikah with the Union Council
  • Application for the NADRA marriage registration certificate

What documents are needed for court marriage?

Keep originals and copies ready on the day.

  • Original CNICs of the bride and groom
  • CNICs of two adult Muslim witnesses
  • Passport-size photographs of both parties
  • Divorce or death certificate if either party was previously married
  • Permission of the Arbitration Council if the husband has an existing wife
  • Passport for foreign nationals or overseas Pakistanis

Why do the nikah nama clauses matter?

The nikah nama is a legal document, and some of its columns affect your rights for years. The amount and form of haq mehr, whether it is prompt or deferred, and whether the wife is given the right of talaq (delegated divorce) are all recorded on it. Many couples sign without reading these columns. Your lawyer will explain each one so that both parties make informed choices.

Is a court marriage legally valid and safe?

Yes. A nikah performed with free consent of adult parties and registered with the Union Council is legally valid. Registration is important: it provides official proof of the marriage for NADRA records, passports, visas and inheritance. If either party faces threats from family because of the marriage, a lawyer can advise on protection, including applications to the court.

Court marriage: frequently asked questions

Can court marriage be done on the same day?

The nikah and affidavits can usually be completed the same day. Union Council registration and the NADRA certificate follow afterwards.

Is court marriage legal without parents' consent?

Under Pakistani law an adult Muslim woman can marry with her own free consent. The nikah must still be properly registered.

What is the minimum age for marriage?

Both parties must be adults. In Islamabad the minimum age for both is 18 under recent federal legislation, and you should confirm the current rule for the province where the nikah is registered.
