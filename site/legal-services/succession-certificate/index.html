Quick answer: succession certificate

A succession certificate lets legal heirs claim a deceased person's bank accounts, shares and other movable assets. Where all heirs agree, it can be obtained from a NADRA Succession Facilitation Unit under the Letters of Administration and Succession Certificates Act 2020 (Islamabad) or the Punjab Act 2021 (Rawalpindi). If heirs disagree, a petition is filed in the civil court under the Succession Act 1925.

Key facts at a glance

  • A succession certificate allows legal heirs to collect a deceased person's movable assets such as bank balances, shares and pensions.
  • Letters of administration deal with the estate more broadly, including immovable property in some cases.
  • In Islamabad, NADRA's Succession Facilitation Units issue certificates for uncontested cases under the Letters of Administration and Succession Certificates Act 2020.
  • Contested matters, and cases outside the NADRA route, are decided by the civil court under the Succession Act 1925 or the applicable provincial law.
  • Shares of heirs are determined according to the personal law of the deceased.

Who needs succession certificate?

Legal heirs of a deceased family member with bank accounts, savings certificates, shares, vehicles or other assets.

What is a succession certificate and why do heirs need one?

When a person dies, banks, companies and government departments will not release their money or assets to the family without legal proof of who the heirs are. A succession certificate is that proof for movable assets. It names the legal heirs and their shares, and it authorises them to collect debts and securities such as bank balances, savings certificates, shares, insurance claims and pension arrears.

Without it, accounts stay frozen, and over time assets can be lost or become harder to recover. Starting the process soon after the death saves time and family disputes.

How do you get a succession certificate in Islamabad?

For uncontested cases in Islamabad, the heirs can apply to NADRA's Succession Facilitation Unit under the Letters of Administration and Succession Certificates Act 2020. NADRA verifies the family tree from its records, the heirs give consent, a public notice process is followed, and the certificate is issued if no objection is raised. This is usually much faster than court proceedings.

If any heir objects, if the family tree in NADRA's records is incomplete, or if the matter is otherwise disputed, the case goes to the civil court. A lawyer helps decide the right route at the start.

How do you get a succession certificate in Rawalpindi?

In Rawalpindi, the route depends on the current Punjab law and whether all heirs agree. Contested matters are decided by the civil court under the Succession Act 1925. The court issues notice to the heirs, publishes a proclamation in a newspaper, hears any objections, and then grants the certificate, usually requiring a surety bond. Your lawyer will advise whether a quicker administrative route is available for your case.

What documents are needed?

Collect as many of these as possible before applying.

  • Death certificate of the deceased issued through NADRA or the Union Council
  • CNIC of the deceased and CNICs or B-forms of all legal heirs
  • Family registration certificate (FRC) from NADRA
  • Details of assets: bank accounts, certificates, shares, insurance and pension
  • Nikah nama of the deceased's spouse where relevant
  • Consent affidavits of heirs, for uncontested applications

How are the shares of heirs decided?

Shares are determined by the personal law of the deceased, which for most Muslims in Pakistan follows the rules of Islamic inheritance applicable to their school of thought. The court or authority calculates the shares of the spouse, children, parents and other relatives. A will cannot override these rules beyond the limits allowed by law. Your lawyer will explain each heir's share before you apply so there are no surprises.

Can heirs living abroad apply?

Yes. Heirs living outside Pakistan can authorise a relative or lawyer through a power of attorney attested at a Pakistani mission, or follow the procedure NADRA allows for overseas applicants. Our lawyers coordinate with all heirs, wherever they live, to keep the process moving.

Succession certificate: frequently asked questions

Which is faster, NADRA or court?

The NADRA route is usually faster when all legal heirs agree. If there is any dispute among heirs, the matter goes to the civil court.

Can a woman heir apply?

Yes. Every legal heir, male or female, is entitled to their share and can participate in the application.

Do I need a succession certificate for property?

Immovable property is usually transferred through inheritance mutation (intiqal) with the land record office. A letter of administration may be needed in some cases.
