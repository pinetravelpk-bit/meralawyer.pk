Quick answer: power of attorney

A power of attorney lets someone act for you. A general power of attorney covers broad matters; a special power of attorney is limited to one task, such as selling a specific property. Overseas Pakistanis sign it before the Pakistani embassy or consulate; for property dealings it is then registered with the sub-registrar in Pakistan.

Key facts at a glance

  • Powers of attorney in Pakistan are governed by the Powers of Attorney Act 1882, with stamp duty under the Stamp Act 1899.
  • A special power of attorney is limited to specific acts; a general power of attorney covers a wider range of acts.
  • A power of attorney used to sell or transfer immovable property generally needs registration under the Registration Act 1908.
  • Overseas Pakistanis can execute a power of attorney at a Pakistani embassy or consulate; stamp duty is then paid in Pakistan.
  • A power of attorney can be revoked, and the revocation should be notified to everyone who relied on it.

Who needs power of attorney?

Overseas Pakistanis, elderly people, and anyone who cannot attend to a matter personally.

What is a power of attorney and when do you need one?

A power of attorney is a document by which you (the principal) authorise another person (the attorney) to act on your behalf. It is used when you cannot be present yourself, for example because you live abroad, are unwell, or simply want a trusted person to handle a transaction. Common uses in Islamabad and Rawalpindi include selling or buying property, managing rent, pursuing a court case, dealing with banks, and completing CDA, society or PLRA formalities.

Special or general power of attorney: which should you choose?

A special power of attorney authorises specific acts, such as selling one identified plot or pursuing one court case. A general power of attorney authorises a wide range of acts over a period of time. For most people, a special power of attorney is safer, because it limits what the attorney can do. General powers are useful in some situations, such as managing all affairs of an elderly parent, but they carry more risk.

  • Use a special power of attorney for a single sale, purchase or case
  • Name the property precisely with plot number, sector or khasra number
  • State whether the attorney can receive money, and into which account
  • Set an end date or end condition where possible
  • Avoid irrevocable powers unless there is a clear legal reason

How is a power of attorney made in Pakistan?

The lawyer drafts the power of attorney on stamp paper of the correct value. The principal signs it before witnesses, and, depending on the purpose, it is attested by an oath commissioner or notary public. If it authorises sale or transfer of immovable property, it is generally registered with the Sub-Registrar, where the principal appears in person and biometric verification may be done.

Some authorities, such as CDA and private housing societies, have their own formats or requirements for powers of attorney, so the lawyer checks these before drafting.

How do overseas Pakistanis make a power of attorney?

If you live abroad, you sign the power of attorney before the Pakistani embassy or consulate, which attests it. Some missions also accept online appointments or specific procedures, so check the mission's requirements. The attested original is then sent to Pakistan, where stamp duty is paid and, if needed, it is registered before it is used. Your lawyer can prepare the wording in advance so that the embassy appointment goes smoothly.

What documents are needed?

Keep these ready for drafting and execution.

  • CNIC or NICOP of the principal
  • CNIC of the attorney
  • Property documents or case details for which the power is given
  • CNICs of two witnesses
  • Passport copy for overseas principals

How do you cancel a power of attorney?

A power of attorney can generally be revoked by the principal. The revocation is made in writing, registered if the original was registered, and notified to the attorney and to any authority or person who may rely on it, such as CDA, the housing society, the Sub-Registrar or a bank. Revocation is especially important if you suspect misuse; act quickly to prevent an unauthorised sale.

Power of attorney: frequently asked questions

Can I sell property through a power of attorney?

Yes, if the POA specifically authorises it and is registered. Buyers and registrars usually require a registered special POA for property sales.

How do I cancel a power of attorney?

Execute a revocation deed, register it where the POA was registered, and notify the attorney and relevant offices.

Is a POA from abroad valid in Pakistan?

Yes, when it is attested by the Pakistani embassy or consulate and, for property, registered in Pakistan.
