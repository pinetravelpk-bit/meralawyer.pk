Why focus on Islamabad and Rawalpindi?

The twin cities share a population and economy but sit in different legal jurisdictions, with different courts, land record systems, rent laws and tax authorities. That creates real confusion for residents, businesses and overseas families. Focusing on these two cities allows MeraLawyer.pk to know the local courts and offices well, from the Islamabad High Court and the district courts in both cities to CDA, PLRA, SECP, FBR and the Punjab Revenue Authority.
