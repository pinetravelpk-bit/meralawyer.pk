What does a property lawyer do?

A property lawyer checks title before you buy, handles transfer and mutation, and takes disputes such as illegal possession, inheritance and rent cases to the civil courts. In Rawalpindi, land records are kept by the Punjab Land Records Authority; in Islamabad, by the ICT revenue office or the CDA, depending on the area. We verify the record before you pay.

Cases our property and civil lawyers handle

Property verification

Title, fard and record checks before you buy.

Transfer and registry

Sale deed, registry and mutation (intiqal).

Illegal possession

Recovery under the Illegal Dispossession Act 2005.

Inheritance and partition

Dividing inherited property between legal heirs.

Frequently asked questions

How do I verify a property before buying in Rawalpindi or Islamabad?

Ask for the fard (record of rights) or society allotment and transfer documents, then have a lawyer verify them with the land record office or housing society, check for pending litigation, and confirm the seller's identity.

What can I do if someone illegally occupies my property?

You can file a complaint under the Illegal Dispossession Act 2005 and, in parallel, a civil suit for possession. Act quickly and keep proof of ownership.

How is inherited property divided?

Shares are fixed by Islamic inheritance law for Muslims. Legal heirs can agree a family settlement, or any heir can file a partition suit in the civil court.
