Family lawyer

A family lawyer represents you in the Family Courts for khula, divorce, child custody, maintenance and dowry recovery. Under the Family Courts Act 1964, these cases are filed in the family court of the area where the wife ordinarily resides. MeraLawyer.pk matches you with a family advocate in Islamabad or Rawalpindi and gives you the full fee before you start.

  • Khula
  • Divorce (talaq)
  • Child custody
  • Maintenance
  • Dowry and gifts recovery
  • Restitution of conjugal rights
  • Court marriage
  • Guardianship certificate

Criminal lawyer

A criminal lawyer protects you from the moment an FIR is registered: applying for pre-arrest or post-arrest bail, challenging a false FIR, and defending you at trial. Bail is sought under sections 497 and 498 of the Code of Criminal Procedure before the Sessions Court or the High Court. Tell us your matter is urgent and we prioritise it.

  • Pre-arrest bail
  • Post-arrest bail
  • FIR registration
  • FIR quashment
  • Trial defence
  • Cyber crime
  • Cheque dishonour
  • Appeals

Property lawyer

A property lawyer checks title before you buy, handles transfer and mutation, and takes disputes such as illegal possession, inheritance and rent cases to the civil courts. In Rawalpindi, land records are kept by the Punjab Land Records Authority; in Islamabad, by the ICT revenue office or the CDA, depending on the area. We verify the record before you pay.

  • Property verification
  • Transfer and registry
  • Illegal possession
  • Inheritance and partition
  • Rent cases
  • Stay orders
  • Specific performance
  • Recovery suits
