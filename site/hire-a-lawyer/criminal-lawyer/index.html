What does a criminal lawyer do?

A criminal lawyer protects you from the moment an FIR is registered: applying for pre-arrest or post-arrest bail, challenging a false FIR, and defending you at trial. Bail is sought under sections 497 and 498 of the Code of Criminal Procedure before the Sessions Court or the High Court. Tell us your matter is urgent and we prioritise it.

Key facts at a glance

  • Bail in non-bailable offences is sought under section 497 (post-arrest) and section 498 (pre-arrest) of the Code of Criminal Procedure 1898.
  • A bail application is normally filed first in the Court of Session, and then in the High Court if refused.
  • A false or malicious FIR can be challenged, including through quashment proceedings before the High Court.
  • Cyber crime offences under the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016 are investigated by the federal cyber crime agency and tried by designated courts.
  • In Rawalpindi, criminal appeals and bail matters beyond the sessions court go to the Lahore High Court, Rawalpindi Bench; in Islamabad, to the Islamabad High Court.

What should you do in the first hours after an FIR is registered?

The first hours matter most. Get a copy of the FIR and note the police station, the sections applied and the names of the complainant and investigating officer. Do not give statements or sign documents without legal advice. If the offence is non-bailable and you have not been arrested, a criminal lawyer can assess whether to apply for pre-arrest bail without delay.

If a family member has already been arrested, the lawyer can check whether the arrest and remand were lawful, apply for post-arrest bail, and make sure the person is produced before a magistrate within the time the law requires. Tell your lawyer at once if the matter is urgent so that it is prioritised.

  • Collect the FIR number, police station and sections of law
  • Do not contact the complainant or witnesses about the case
  • Keep documents, messages and receipts that support your side
  • Share any medical reports or evidence of an earlier dispute
  • Call a lawyer before appearing at the police station

What is the difference between pre-arrest and post-arrest bail?

Pre-arrest bail (also called protective or anticipatory bail) under section 498 CrPC protects a person from arrest. Courts grant it when the accused shows that the case appears to be motivated by mala fide intentions or ulterior motives, along with the usual grounds for bail. Post-arrest bail under section 497 CrPC is for a person already in custody, and the court considers the nature of the offence, the evidence and whether the case calls for further inquiry.

Offences that are bailable under the Code are different: the accused has a right to bail under section 496. A criminal lawyer reviews the sections in your FIR to tell you which route applies and how strong the grounds are.

Which courts hear criminal matters in Islamabad and Rawalpindi?

In Islamabad, criminal trials and bail applications start in the District and Sessions Courts (East and West), with further remedies in the Islamabad High Court. In Rawalpindi, they start at the district courts at Kachehri, with further remedies in the Lahore High Court, Rawalpindi Bench. Special courts hear particular categories, such as anti-corruption, banking and cyber crime cases, and anti-terrorism matters are heard by anti-terrorism courts.

Choosing a lawyer who appears regularly in the relevant court is practical as well as legal: they know the procedure, the listing system and how to move an urgent application on the same day.

How can a false FIR be challenged?

Several remedies exist, and the right one depends on the facts. The accused can join the investigation and produce evidence of innocence, ask senior police officers to change the investigation, seek bail, or apply to the High Court to quash the FIR or proceedings where the case is clearly an abuse of process. The complainant in a false case can also face legal consequences under the Pakistan Penal Code.

A careful lawyer will not promise quashment. They will explain which remedy has the best chance on your facts and what evidence will help, such as call records, CCTV footage, documents showing you were elsewhere, or proof of a prior civil dispute.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between pre-arrest and post-arrest bail?

Pre-arrest bail (section 498 CrPC) protects you from arrest before it happens and is granted only in limited circumstances, such as a malicious case. Post-arrest bail (section 497 CrPC) is sought after you have been arrested.

Can I get bail on the same day?

A bail petition can often be filed the same day, but the court decides when to hear it. In urgent matters an interim pre-arrest bail may be granted at the first hearing.

What should I do if a false FIR is registered against me?

Do not ignore it. Speak to a criminal lawyer immediately about pre-arrest bail and whether the FIR can be challenged through a quashment petition.

Do you handle FIA cyber crime cases?

Yes. Our criminal lawyers handle complaints and defence in FIA cyber crime cases under PECA 2016.
