What does a family lawyer do?

A family lawyer represents you in the Family Courts for khula, divorce, child custody, maintenance and dowry recovery. Under the Family Courts Act 1964, these cases are filed in the family court of the area where the wife ordinarily resides. MeraLawyer.pk matches you with a family advocate in Islamabad or Rawalpindi and gives you the full fee before you start.

Key facts at a glance

  • Family cases in Islamabad and Rawalpindi are heard by the Family Courts under the Family Courts Act 1964.
  • A khula, maintenance or dowry suit is usually filed where the wife ordinarily resides.
  • Family courts are directed to decide cases within six months of the defendant's first appearance (section 12-A).
  • Divorce and khula take effect through the Union Council or Arbitration Council after the 90-day period under the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961.
  • Child custody and guardianship are decided under the Guardians and Wards Act 1890, with the welfare of the child as the main test.

When should you hire a family lawyer in Islamabad or Rawalpindi?

You should speak to a family lawyer as soon as a marriage, custody or maintenance dispute becomes difficult to settle within the family. Early advice protects your position: a lawyer can tell you which court has jurisdiction, what evidence to preserve (nikah nama, receipts for dowry articles, bank transfers, messages) and whether a negotiated settlement is realistic before anything is filed.

Many people wait until they receive a court summons or a talaq notice. By then, deadlines are running and documents may be lost. A short consultation at the start usually costs far less than repairing a case later, and it gives you a clear picture of the time and fees involved in Islamabad or Rawalpindi.

How does a family case move through the Family Court?

A family suit starts with a plaint filed in the Family Court that has territorial jurisdiction. The court issues summons, the defendant files a written statement, and the judge holds a pre-trial hearing to attempt reconciliation. If reconciliation fails, the court frames issues, records evidence from both sides and then decides the case. In a khula suit, the court can pass a decree at the pre-trial stage if reconciliation fails.

After a decree, the work is not always finished. Maintenance decrees often need execution proceedings to recover money, and custody orders may need to be enforced. Your lawyer should explain these later stages at the start so you know the full path, not only the first hearing.

Which family court will hear your case: Islamabad or Rawalpindi?

Jurisdiction mostly depends on where the wife ordinarily resides, or in custody matters, where the minor ordinarily lives. If the wife lives in an Islamabad sector such as G-9, I-8 or F-10, the case usually goes to the Family Courts in Islamabad. If she lives in Satellite Town, Saddar, Chaklala or Bahria Town Rawalpindi, the case usually goes to the Family Courts at the Rawalpindi district courts.

Filing in the wrong court causes delays and can lead to the plaint being returned. Because the twin cities sit side by side and many families move between them, checking jurisdiction carefully is one of the first things a family lawyer in Islamabad or Rawalpindi does.

What documents should you bring to a family lawyer?

Bring whatever you have; your lawyer will tell you what else to collect. Copies are enough for the first meeting, but keep originals safe because the court may ask to see them.

  • Nikah nama (marriage certificate) and NADRA marriage registration certificate, if issued
  • CNICs of both spouses and B-forms or birth certificates of children
  • Any talaq notice, khula notice or Union Council correspondence
  • List and receipts of dowry articles, jewellery and gifts
  • Evidence of income or expenses relevant to maintenance (salary slips, school fee receipts, medical bills)
  • Messages, call records or letters relevant to the dispute

How much does a family lawyer cost, and what affects the fee?

There is no fixed government rate for lawyers' fees in Pakistan. The fee depends on the type of case, whether it is contested, the number of hearings expected and whether execution or appeal is likely. An uncontested khula or a court marriage is simpler than a contested custody case with several witnesses.

Can overseas Pakistanis run a family case without travelling?

Yes, in most cases. You can appoint a lawyer and a local attorney through a power of attorney attested at a Pakistani embassy or consulate. The lawyer files and attends hearings, and you follow progress through WhatsApp updates and video calls. Some stages, such as recording your own evidence, may need your presence or special arrangements, and your lawyer will tell you in advance if that is the case.
