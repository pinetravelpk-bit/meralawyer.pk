Privacy policy

Reviewed by [Advocate name], Advocate High CourtLast updated 25 October 2026

Key facts at a glance

  • MeraLawyer.pk collects only the information needed to understand your matter and contact you.
  • Information is shared with the case manager and the lawyer handling your matter, and is not sold.
  • You can ask for access, correction or deletion of your information by email.
  • The website uses a minimal amount of JavaScript and does not require an account to use.

Who is responsible for your information?

MeraLawyer.pk is responsible for personal information collected through this website and through our phone, WhatsApp and email channels. If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us using the email address on the contact page.

What information do we collect?

We collect information that you choose to give us, and limited technical information needed to run the website.

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, WhatsApp number and email address
  • Matter details: your city, the type of matter, and any description you provide
  • Documents you send us, such as copies of agreements, notices or court papers
  • Communication records: messages and call notes relating to your matter
  • Technical data: standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, kept for security and to keep the site working

How do we use your information?

We use your information to understand your legal matter, match you with a suitable lawyer, contact you about it, provide the services you request, keep records required for professional and legal purposes, and improve our service. We do not use the details of your matter for advertising and we do not sell personal information.

How does the WhatsApp form work?

The contact forms on this website do not send your details to our server. When you press send, your browser opens WhatsApp with your details already filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. Messages you send through WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

Who do we share your information with?

Your information is shared only as needed to handle your matter.

  • The lawyer matched to your matter, who is bound by professional confidentiality
  • Case managers who coordinate your matter
  • Government bodies, courts or authorities where needed to perform the service you asked for, for example SECP, FBR or a court
  • Service providers such as hosting or communication tools, only to the extent needed to operate
  • Authorities where disclosure is required by law

How long do we keep your information?

We keep information for as long as needed to handle your matter and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet professional, legal and record-keeping requirements and to deal with any follow-up questions. When information is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised.

How do we protect your information?

The website is served over HTTPS. Access to client information is limited to people who need it for your matter. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please share only what is needed and use the official contact details shown on this website.

What are your choices and rights?

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to any records we must keep by law or for an ongoing matter. You can also ask us to stop contacting you. Send your request to the email address on the contact page and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Does this website use cookies or tracking?

The website is built without a tracking framework and works without JavaScript. If we add analytics or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy to explain what they collect and how you can opt out.

What is the legal basis for using your information?

We use your information because you have asked us to help with a legal matter, and we need it to provide that help. We also keep certain records because professional and legal obligations require it, and we use limited technical information to keep the website secure and working. If we ever want to use your information for a different purpose, such as sending you newsletters, we will ask you first, and you can say no without affecting your matter.

Do we send marketing messages?

We do not add you to marketing lists because you contacted us about a legal matter. Messages you receive from us will relate to your matter, such as hearing updates, document requests or fee confirmations. If you ever receive a message you did not expect, or one that claims to be from MeraLawyer.pk but uses different contact details, please tell us.

What about information you share about other people?

Legal matters often involve other people, such as a spouse, a tenant, a business partner or the other side in a dispute. Please share only what is needed for us and the lawyer to understand your matter. We use that information only for handling your matter and keep it confidential in the same way as your own information.

How do we handle information about children?

Family matters can involve children, for example in custody or maintenance cases. We collect information about children only from their parent or guardian and only as needed for the matter, such as names, ages and B-form details required by the court. This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information directly from them.

What if you contact us from outside Pakistan?

Many clients are overseas Pakistanis. If you contact us from abroad, your information will be handled in Pakistan by the case manager and lawyer working on your matter, and may pass through communication services such as WhatsApp and email that operate internationally. By contacting us from abroad you understand that your information will be processed in this way for the purpose of handling your matter.

How do you contact us about privacy?

Send any privacy question or request to the email address on our contact page, with the words 'privacy request' in the subject. Tell us your name and the phone number or email you used to contact us, so that we can find your records. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request, to protect your information from anyone else.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the date it was last reviewed. Significant changes will be highlighted on the website.
